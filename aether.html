Before you begin
This app is for relaxation and personal wellness only. It doesn't diagnose, treat, or cure anything.
Seizure warning. Sessions use rhythmic sound pulses. If you have epilepsy, photosensitivity, or a seizure history, talk to a doctor before using.
Consult a healthcare professional before use if you have any medical condition.
Start at low volume with short sessions. Stop if you feel unwell or get a headache.
Use stereo headphones — binaural and isochronic tones need them.
