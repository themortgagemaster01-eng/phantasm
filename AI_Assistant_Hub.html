Post-Mortem / Continuous Feedback Template
Run bi-weekly with engineering & product teams. Blameless and action-focused.
  • What went well? Celebrate wins and note what to replicate.
  • What could be improved? Specific and actionable, not personal.
  • Root cause analysis. 5 Whys (or similar) on the biggest issue.
  • Action items. Task — Owner — Due date — Priority.
  • Follow-up checkpoint. When will we confirm these are done?
Cross-Training Program Outline
Reduce key-person risk on critical infrastructure roles.
  • Identify critical roles and current single points of failure (key-person risk).
  • Design 4–6 week rotational modules per role pairing.
  • Include shadowing, documentation handoff, and live knowledge-transfer sessions.
  • Measure: reduced key-person-dependency score + internal mobility / promotion rate.
Governance & Next Steps
  • Assign an executive sponsor per pillar (target: within 14 business days).
  • Stand up a cross-functional steering committee; meet bi-weekly to start.
  • Define baseline metrics immediately for the mandate targets.
  • Launch the two quick wins in parallel for early momentum.
  • Pilot AI workflows using the Prompt Library tab — approved tools, human-in-the-loop.
